## 4. interpretation

The bottleneck is now downstream of recent-impact observability.

A real recent impact can be decoded from phase-residualized MaleCNS activity with approximately 90% balanced accuracy on TRAIN, EVAL, and fresh HOLDOUT. However, summing rectified detector margin frame-by-frame does not stably reconstruct cumulative injury.

The next diagnostic should attribute accumulator failure without retuning the D6 detector. In particular it should measure:

- background false-positive evidence;
- lingering post-impact evidence;
- repeated frames generated by one physical impact;
- evidence amplitude per physical impact;
- how much accumulated evidence is attributable to true impact episodes versus background leakage.

This attribution must remain evaluator-only. It may motivate a later neural eventizer, but D6 oracle-labelled detector weights themselves remain non-deployable.
